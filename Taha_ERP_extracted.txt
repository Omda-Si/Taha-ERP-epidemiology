Feature Proposal: Epidemiological Data
Sync (Draft)
System: Clinic & Hospital ERP System

Prepared For: Taha Group

Status: DRAFT - PENDING CLIENT REVIEW

1. Executive Summary
Objective: To implement a secure, automated feature within the existing Clinic & Hospital ERP
system that aggregates and transmits anonymized epidemiological data to a central Taha
Group server.

As the primary ERP system manages daily clinic operations—including patient registration,
medical history, doctor scheduling, and billing—it naturally collects valuable public health and
demographic data. This proposal outlines a feature to extract specific, non-identifiable data
points regarding disease prevalence and demographics to assist Taha Group in macro-level
health trend analysis and resource planning.

2. Core ERP Context
To ensure this feature integrates smoothly, it will operate silently alongside the standard ERP
modules currently used by clinics and hospitals:
 ●​ Patient Management: Registration, visit history, and appointment tracking.
 ●​ Clinical Records: Doctor notes, diagnoses, and treatment plans.
 ●​ Facility Operations: Department routing, pharmacy integration, and billing.
The proposed data sync feature will sit beneath these modules, securely reading diagnostic
codes and demographics without interrupting the medical staff's daily workflow.

3. Proposed Feature: Centralized Anonymized Sync
When a physician logs a diagnosis (e.g., Malaria, Influenza, Diabetes) into the patient's record,
the system will trigger a background process. This process strips all Personal Identifiable
Information (PII) from the record, packages the generalized demographic and medical data,
and sends it to the Taha Group main server.

3.1 Data Inclusion & Exclusion Strict Guidelines
Patient privacy is the highest priority. The system will strictly filter data before any transmission
occurs.

Data Sent to Main Server (Anonymized):
 ●​   Disease/Diagnosis: Standard medical code.
 ●​   Age Bracket / Exact Age: (e.g., 34, or 30-40).
 ●​   Occupation/Type of Work: (e.g., Construction, Teacher).
 ●​   General Location: City, District, or Region.
 ●​   Timestamp: Date of diagnosis.
Data Blocked / Excluded (Private):
 ●​   Patient Names: First, Last, or Aliases.
 ●​   Contact Info: Phone numbers, emails.
 ●​   Exact Addresses: Street names, house numbers.
 ●​   Identification: National ID, Passport, or System ID.
 ●​   Financials: Billing info, insurance details.

4. Business Value for Taha Group
 ●​ Epidemiological Tracking: Identify disease outbreaks in specific regions or among
    specific age groups in real-time.
 ●​ Resource Allocation: If a specific clinic is seeing a spike in a certain disease, Taha Group
    can preemptively route medical supplies and specialists to that area.
 ●​ Occupational Health Insights: Correlate specific health issues with patient occupations
    to inform preventative health campaigns.

6. Technical Implementation Notes
To ensure system stability, the feature will be designed with the following technical
considerations:
 ●​ Asynchronous Processing: The data sync will not slow down the doctor's application. If
    the clinic loses internet connection, the system will securely store the anonymized data
    locally and sync it automatically once the connection is restored.
 ●​ Encryption: Even though the data is anonymized, all transmissions to the Taha Group
    central server will be encrypted.
